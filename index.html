FAQ

Common questions.

What's included in a standard cleaning?
Our standard clean covers all main living areas, kitchens, and bathrooms — including dusting, vacuuming, mopping, surface sanitizing, trash removal, and tidy-up of visible spaces. For things like inside the oven, inside the fridge, baseboards, blinds, and interior windows, book a deep clean.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes — we arrive fully equipped with professional-grade vacuums, microfiber cloths, mops, and our standard eco-conscious cleaning products. If you'd prefer we use your products (for any reason — allergies, finishes, scent preferences), just let us know in the notes.
Are your products safe for pets and children?
Yes. We use plant-based, non-toxic products by default. They're safe around children, pets, and people with sensitivities. If a specific surface needs something stronger (heavy mildew, baked-on grease), we'll tell you before we use it.
What's the difference between a standard and deep cleaning?
A standard clean keeps an already-maintained space looking great. A deep clean is for spaces that haven't been cleaned in a while, or before a move, baby, or big event. Deep cleans include:
  • Inside the oven, microwave, and fridge
  • Baseboards, door frames, and light fixtures
  • Interior windows and tracks
  • Grout and tile attention
  • Ceiling fans and vents
How does recurring cleaning work?
Choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. We assign you a dedicated team that learns your space and preferences, and we lock in a recurring day and time window. You can pause, reschedule, or cancel anytime — no contracts. Recurring visits start from $100/visit (weekly) because we're maintaining a space that's already been reset.
Do you handle move-in and move-out cleans?
Yes — these are deep cleans of an empty (or near-empty) space, top to bottom. We focus on the things landlords, buyers, and inspectors look at: inside cabinets and drawers, appliances, baseboards, blinds, and bathrooms. Tell us if you need a receipt or before/after photos and we'll send them.
How early or late can you book?
We offer same-day availability, rush cleaning, and last-minute bookings based on team availability. The earlier you reach out, the more time slots we have open — but if you need someone today, call us at (945) 383-2368 and we'll see what we can do.
Are you insured? What if something is damaged?
Yes — we're fully insured. Damage is rare, but if it happens we'll let you know before you find it, document it, and make it right. No phone tag, no deflecting.
